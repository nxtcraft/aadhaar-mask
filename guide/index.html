What is Aadhaar Masking?

Aadhaar masking replaces the first 8 digits of a 12-digit Aadhaar number with X or # characters, showing only the last 4 digits. Example: 1234 5678 9012 → XXXX XXXX 9012.

UIDAI introduced this in 2018 to prevent misuse of Aadhaar numbers. A masked Aadhaar is a valid identity proof accepted by banks, insurance companies and telecom operators.

When to Use Masked Aadhaar

  • Bank account KYC updates
  • Insurance policy applications
  • Mobile SIM registration
  • Rental agreements
  • Any form where you are asked to submit a photocopy of Aadhaar

Accepted Formats

  • XXXX XXXX 9012 — most common, accepted by all entities
  • #### #### 9012 — hash format, also accepted
  • Masked Aadhaar PDF from uidai.gov.in — digitally signed, most authoritative

How to Get Official Masked Aadhaar from UIDAI

  1. Go to uidai.gov.in → Download Aadhaar.
  2. Log in with your Aadhaar number and OTP.
  3. Check the option "Masked Aadhaar" before downloading.
  4. The downloaded PDF shows only last 4 digits and carries UIDAI's digital signature.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I mask my Aadhaar number?

Sharing the full 12-digit Aadhaar exposes it to identity theft. UIDAI guidelines say only the last 4 digits should be displayed. Masked Aadhaar (XXXX XXXX 1234) is accepted for KYC by all regulated entities.

Is masked Aadhaar accepted for KYC?

Yes. As per UIDAI circulars, masked Aadhaar is a valid KYC document for banks (RBI-regulated), insurance (IRDAI), and telecom (TRAI). Entities cannot legally demand the full 12-digit number.

Does this tool store my Aadhaar number?

No. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your Aadhaar number never leaves your device. There is no backend server or database.
